# Policy Evidence Review

1. Confirm the material set, applicable organization, effective date, and requested output.
2. Extract explicit requirements, responsible roles, time limits, prerequisites, exceptions, and approval gates.
3. Attach a source identifier and exact passage location to every factual conclusion.
4. Mark missing evidence as `待补证`, conflicting passages as `存在冲突`, and analyst judgment as `分析判断`.
5. Produce a draft with: task scope, verified facts, requirements table, risks/open questions, sources, and human-review checklist.
6. Never treat the draft as an official decision. Require an authorized person to review the source, applicability, and timeliness before approval.
